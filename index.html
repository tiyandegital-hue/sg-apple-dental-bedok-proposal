FINDING F-01CRITICAL SEVERITYREMEDIATION TARGET: TIER 1 BASIC

No website exists under any owned domain

VERIFIED EVIDENCE

Exact-name search for “Apple Dental Clinic Bedok” returns zero owned web properties. The only search results surfaced are government and aggregator listings (MOH HealthHub, CPF, SingHealth).

WHY IT COSTS PATIENTS

Patients researching a local clinic look for an official website to confirm legitimacy, hours, and services before making contact. With no website at all, absence reads as an inactive or closed clinic.

ONE-LINE FIX

Deploy a dedicated, mobile-first clinic domain with structured schema and verified hours.

FINDING F-02CRITICAL SEVERITYREMEDIATION TARGET: TIER 1 BASIC

VERIFIED EVIDENCE

No claimed GBP knowledge panel, verified rating, or review count appears for queries on Google Maps Bedok North. Profile status: [TO CONFIRM — may exist unmanaged or unclaimed].

WHY IT COSTS PATIENTS

Without a managed GBP listing, the clinic cannot appear in Google Maps results or the Local Pack where most “dentist Bedok” decisions are made.

ONE-LINE FIX

Claim or create official GBP verification for 416 Bedok North Ave 2, populate complete NAP data, and verify category as “Dental clinic”.

FINDING F-03CRITICAL SEVERITYREMEDIATION TARGET: TIER 2 STANDARD

Zero visibility in AI engine answers (GEO Deficit)

VERIFIED EVIDENCE

Queries on ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google Gemini (“best MediSave dentist in Bedok”, “dentist open Saturday Bedok”) return zero citations to Apple Dental Clinic. AI engines cite Casa Dental, Teeth Q, and Dental Designs instead.

WHY IT COSTS PATIENTS

Generative AI search synthesizes local recommendations directly. If an LLM cannot ingest verified hours, services, and location data, the clinic is excluded from the answer entirely — before the patient ever sees a list.

ONE-LINE FIX

Publish citable FAQ architecture, LocalBusiness JSON-LD markup, and machine-readable llms.txt containing verified hours, MediSave status, and address.

FINDING F-04HIGH SEVERITYREMEDIATION TARGET: TIER 1 BASIC

No services, prices, or dentist profile anywhere public

VERIFIED EVIDENCE

The only public service description across all official directories is generic “Outpatient Dental Service”. No treatment list, no price list, and no dentist bio or credentials were found anywhere public.

WHY IT COSTS PATIENTS

Dental anxiety is strongly tied to price uncertainty and clinician unfamiliarity. Without knowing what is treated, what it costs, or who treats them, patients shortlist clinics that show both.

ONE-LINE FIX

Publish a verified treatment catalog with indicative price bands and a dentist profile — real treatments and prices supplied by the clinic.

FINDING F-05HIGH SEVERITYREMEDIATION TARGET: TIER 2 STANDARD

VERIFIED EVIDENCE

The only published contact paths are landline 6441 6880 and a personal Gmail address (Liuyonglow@gmail.com). Zero online calendar, zero WhatsApp Business line. No WhatsApp presence verified.

WHY IT COSTS PATIENTS

Patients research and try to book outside opening hours — after 18:00 and on Sundays — when the clinic cannot be reached by phone. Every booking today requires a call inside 09:00–17:00 weekday hours.

Integrate an asynchronous 3-step appointment intake form and a dedicated WhatsApp Business button.
